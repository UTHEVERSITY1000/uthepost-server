Frequently Asked Questions & Pro Tips

❓ Why do signups require a company email domain?
To prevent spam and protect job seekers, U-THEPOST requires legitimate corporate/company domains for employer registration. Personal email services (@gmail, @yahoo) are not allowed.
❓ How does Top-of-Page Spotlight work?
When candidates search matching keywords or locations on u-theJOBS, Spotlight listings are dynamically sorted to position #1 with a distinctive gold badge.
❓ Can I edit a post after sharing to social media?
Third-party social networks (X, LinkedIn) do not permit external websites to modify published posts. Use ✏️ Edit & Re-Share in Tab 2 to broadcast an updated post live.
❓ How do I schedule interviews with applicants?
Open any candidate card in Tab 3, tap 📅 Propose Date & Time, choose your preferred slot, and the meeting proposal is sent directly to the candidate's inbox.
